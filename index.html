India Homes Grid

Homes per 100 m square in the 100 largest cities, from satellite building traces and the population grid. Built 2026-10-13 from free data.

Localities in
Homes = people ÷ state household size; people 2025 are modelled (GHSL, EU JRC, projected from 2018 imagery). Buildings are observed (Google Open Buildings, 2023). Squares of 100 m, shown here summed to 500 m. Two cities use buildings × 1.1 because the population grid under-counts them. Satellite towns share squares with their metro. Wired-line signal = Ookla open data, Jul–Sep 2026, 600 m tiles, free to study, Ookla licence for commercial use. M-Lab = public statistics by city (IP location), 2023 to 26 Mar 2024, where the public files stop.
